Light as a feather.

A minimal, ad-free home screen for Android TV and Google TV. Your apps and what you were watching. Nothing else.

DownloadSource code

Tiny and fast

A 0.4 MB app with no third-party libraries and no extra background services.

No ads, no tracking

No sponsored rows, no account, no analytics. Ever.

Continue Watching

Resume shows and films from your apps, right on the home screen.

Clean apps row

Monochrome or colour logos. Reorder, sort by most used, hide, or lock with a PIN.

Home button

The Home button on your remote opens Plume, even on Google TV.

Your look

Light, dark or automatic theme, your own photos, weather and a gallery screensaver.
